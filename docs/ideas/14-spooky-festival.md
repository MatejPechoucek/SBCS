# 14 - The Spooky Festival

My idea, and timely (it is late October). The Spooky Festival has its own rolls: **Trick or Treat chests**, bat drops,
Spooky fishing (Phantom Fisher, Grim Reaper - see [06](06-water-mythics.md)) and the Headless Horseman.

## Rolls

**Theory, from memory - read the wiki.** Trick or Treat chests (fandom has a page) give candy and occasionally rare
items; the Headless Horseman (summoned with a Horseman's Candle) drops the Horseman's set and rare pets; Fear Mongerer
shop items come from candy, not rolls.

## Detection

**Theory.** Chest and drop lines in chat (`RARE DROP!` already recorded). Only active during the festival.

## Cutscene concept: "Trick or Treat"

A carved pumpkin in the corner, its face flickering; it either laughs and spits candy (the common case, half a second)
or its eyes go wide and the rare item rises out of it in purple flame.
