# 16 - Hunting: shard fusion

My idea. The Hunting skill (2025-26) brought Attribute Shards: charmed, black-holed or fished up, and **fused** in the
Fusion Machine into higher shards. Some fusions have random results, and the best shards are expensive - a roll the
player triggers on purpose, over and over.

## Detection

**Theory.** Fusion results show in the Fusion Machine menu and probably in chat. Charm lines are already parsed
(`LavaDrops.charmOf`, "CHARM! You charmed the X and received ..."). Read SkyHanni/Skyblocker's shard trackers for the
strings, and the wiki for which fusions are random.

## Cutscene concept

A tiny one: the two input shards orbit and collide in the corner, a flash in the result's rarity colour, the shard
drops out; a rare result gets a ring and its price.
