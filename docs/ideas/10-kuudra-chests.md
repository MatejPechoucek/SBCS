# 10 - Kuudra chests

My idea. Kuudra runs end with a Free and a Paid chest (opened with a Kuudra Key), and the paid chest is a roll with real
jackpots - exactly the Ossuary's situation (dungeon chests, draft PR #2).

## Trigger

Opening the Paid chest at the end of an Infernal (or chosen tier) run.

## Drops

**Theory, from memory** - research the loot table before planning: Infernal tier armour pieces with attributes
(the attribute roll is the real RNG - godrolls), Hollow Wand / Kuudra weapons, Enrager, Wheel of Fate, Burning Kuudra
Core, Tentacle Dye (the rarest), Kuudra Teeth, essence.

Since the 2025-26 attribute rework (shards, Hunting) the attribute economy has changed - check what makes a chest
valuable today before designing tiers.

## Detection

**Theory.** As the Ossuary: read the chest menu's items when it opens (`ChestReader`'s approach), never click for the
player, cover the menu while the cutscene plays. Strings from Skyblocker/SkyHanni's Kuudra profit trackers (facts
only).

## Cutscene concept

Kuudra is already in Eruption (Kuudra Rises, the Carmine Dye ending), with its rig and tentacles - reuse them: the
chest sits on the lava, tentacles coil round it, the lock glows through heat colours (the Eruption tell), and for a
Tentacle Dye Kuudra itself rises behind the chest.
