# 15 - Glacite Tunnels: fossils and mineshafts

My idea. The Thaw already covers Vanguard corpses, and the other corpses are never animated (Matej's rule). Two other
Glacite Tunnels rolls are left:

1. **Finding a Glacite Mineshaft.** Mining in the tunnels has a small chance to open a mineshaft, and a Vanguard
   mineshaft is the one everyone wants (that is where Vanguard corpses come from). The mineshaft's *type* is a roll.
2. **Fossils.** Mining can expose fossil blocks; Fossil Excavation (Dr. Stone) turns a fossil into a reward, the rarest
   being fossil pets / big Fossil Dust payouts.

## Detection

**Theory.** A mineshaft spawn is announced in chat (from memory "WOW! You found a Glacite Mineshaft portal!") and its
type is shown on entering (sidebar or chat). Fossil excavation is a menu (Fossil Excavator), readable as the Ossuary
reads chests. Records first; the wiki's Glacite Tunnels page did not load on 2026-10-23.

## Cutscene concept

- **Mineshaft:** a short "drill" beat - the ice in front of the player cracks in a ring, the portal's colour shows
  through a hole that widens; Vanguard's colour (the Thaw's blue-gold) is the jackpot, and it hands on naturally to
  the Thaw later in the same shaft.
- **Fossil:** a brush sweeps dust off a block in the corner until the bones show, the rare fossil's outline glows.
