# 08 - Zealots and the Summoning Eye

My idea. The classic SkyBlock grind: Zealots in the End, hunting the **Special Zealot** that drops a Summoning Eye.

## Trigger

A Special Zealot kill (it always drops the Eye), and the Pearlescent Dye from any Zealot.

## Drops (wiki, 2026-10-23)

| Event | Chance |
| --- | --- |
| Special Zealot spawns after a Zealot kill | ~1 in 420 (from memory; the wiki page linked from Zealot has it) with a **pity at 400 kills** |
| Summoning Eye | always, from the Special Zealot |
| Ender Chest Zealot (~2%) | one item from Ench. Ender Pearl / End Stone / Obsidian / Crystal Fragment / Eye of Ender |
| Pearlescent Dye | 0.00001% per Zealot |

## Detection

**Theory.** Not to be confused with the Ender Chest Zealot (the one holding a chest). The Special Zealot is announced
in chat when it spawns (from memory) and its drop is a `RARE DROP! Summoning Eye` line. Simplest: trigger on the drop
line only, as Fin does. The outcome is known, so it is a short popup, not a pending cutscene.

## Cutscene concept: "The Eye" popup (~3 s)

An enderman silhouette in the dimmed corner tears in half (the Zealot), a purple pixel vortex, and the Summoning Eye
opens - lid parts, the pupil looks left, right, at the player. Kill count since the last Eye ("after 386 Zealots")
from SbcsStats, which is what Zealot grinders actually care about. Pearlescent Dye gets a full stopped-time ending
(the eight End Crystal beams of the Seraph's scene converging).
