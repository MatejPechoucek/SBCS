## Cutscene concept: "The Deep"

A single fishing scene with per-creature dressing:

- The camera looks down through water (a pixel floor like Eruption's, but blue, with caustics); a bobber at the
  centre. The creature's silhouette circles below - the only per-creature part, and it is known to the player
  already, so it tells nothing (Diana's rule: the creature is not the verdict).
- **Tell:** the line goes taut and the bobber is pulled under in pulses; the water's colour cycles through a fixed
  set; the reveal lands on a colour. Bust: the line snaps. Win: the drop is reeled up through the water into the
  showcase. The Aquamarine Dye (Titanoboa, Wiki Tiki, Nessie... share it) gets The Bayou Floods from 03.
- Seasonal creatures (Yeti, Reindrake, Grim Reaper, Phantom Fisher) could reskin the water: ice for winter, black
  water and fog for Spooky.
