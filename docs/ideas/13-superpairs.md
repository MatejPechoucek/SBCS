# 13 - Experimentation Table (Superpairs)

My idea. The Experimentation Table's Superpairs is a literal memory/luck game whose prize is **whatever you uncover** -
big enchanted books (and at the top tier, the famous ones). The reward is a roll, and the table is already a screen
the player is staring at.

## Trigger

Claiming a Superpairs reward above a price floor (e.g. an Enchanted Book worth 1M+).

## Rolls

**Theory.** The rewards depend on the table tier (Metaphysical highest) and are visible as the player uncovers them,
so the "roll" is partly skill. Read the wiki's Superpairs page for the reward pool and odds.

## Detection

**Theory.** The game is a chest-style menu (`Superpairs (Metaphysical)`): the uncovered items are readable as the
Ossuary reads chest menus. The claim is a menu click by the player - never click for them.

## Cutscene concept

Not a takeover - the player is in a menu. A small flourish over the menu when the best card flips: the card glows, the
book opens and shines in its rarity's colour, the price counts up.

## Open questions

- Is this "RNG" enough for Matej, given it is a memory game? It may fit better as a lighter "nice find" effect.
