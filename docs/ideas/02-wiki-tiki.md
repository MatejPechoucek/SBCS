# 02 - Wiki Tiki

Matej's idea. A Mythic sea creature found only in **Hotspots** (any water), Fishing 40+, 75M HP, a Construct - a tiki
totem.

## Trigger

Every kill of our own Wiki Tiki, pending on the kill, as Titanoboa (03).

## Drops (wiki, 2026-10-23)

| Drop | Chance | Tier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enchanted Jungle Wood, Enchanted Clay Ball x8-16 | 100% | bust |
| Enchanted Clay Block | 1% | bust (check price) |

Catch rate: hotspot creatures override a catch 15% of the time (+10% Hotspot Tonic Mixin, +20% Hotspot Hook, +20%
Hotspot Bait, +20% Hermit Crab pet); Wiki Tiki is 0.25% of that pool (weight 25).

## Detection

- **Catch line (wiki):** "The water bubbles and froths. A massive form emerges- you have disturbed the Wiki Tiki! You
  shall pay the price."
- Ours / dead / drop line: the shared fishing watcher from 03.
- **Invulnerable phases (wiki):** it summons Laser Totems (5 HP, true melee only) and cannot be damaged while any is up.
  Like Jawbus floating up (0.2.83), its nametag may change or hide its HP during that phase - the watcher must keep
  following the entity whatever its name says. **Theory** until recorded. The totems are separate mobs with their
  own nametags; they must never be mistaken for the Tiki (they are named "Laser Totem" on the wiki).
- **Compacting:** it turns blocks to terracotta in four stages (lime, yellow, orange, red). Purely visual, not
  detection - but it is the obvious tell (below).

## Cutscene concept: "The Totem"

The Tiki's own mechanic becomes the tell. A three-head-high totem stands in the centre on a ring of blocks.

- **Build-up:** the ring compacts in Hypixel's own stages - lime, yellow, orange, red - and back round, faster each
  lap (the Hellion shield cycle), the totem's carved faces lighting one by one bottom-up; laser beams from two small
  totems at the sides sweep across it.
- **Reveal:** the lasers converge. Bust: the totem cracks and topples into clay balls and jungle wood. Troubled Bubble:
  the beams are swallowed by a bubble forming in the top face's mouth, which floats free. **Tiki Mask:** the top face
  peels off the totem, turns to face the camera, and its eyes light gold - the mask. **Aquamarine Dye:** the ring
  floods aquamarine instead of red, the totem drowns and the dye rises out of the water.
